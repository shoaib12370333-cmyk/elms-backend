Uses the same Extension Key as the ELMS Amazon Importer. Don’t have one?
  • 1In ELMS → Orders, click the AMAZON link on the order you're about to place.
  • 2A small panel shows the buyer's address - copy each field and paste it onto Amazon's own form yourself.
  • 3Complete the purchase on Amazon in that same tab, exactly as you normally would.
  • 4Once Amazon shows your order confirmation, ELMS saves the delivery date and buying cost on that order by itself.
